## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a modern, approachable, and immaculately fresh visual identity tailored for an elevated neighborhood laundry service. The aesthetic balances the crisp purity of clean textiles with the warm, reassuring hospitality of a local care provider. 

Drawing from modern minimalism infused with soft tactile warmth and delicate organic forms, the interface avoids the cold sterility of commercial utilities. Instead, it invites trust and relaxation through airy whitespace, gentle bubbly geometries, soothing pastel gradients, and tactile, pillowy interactions. The emotional tone is breezy, calming, dependable, and delightfully clean.

## Colors

The palette revolves around clean water, fresh floral fabric softeners, and sunlit cotton sheets:

- **Primary (`#7B68EE` / `#9D8DF1` / `#E8E5F8`)**: Soft lavender and lilac accents evoke floral freshness and calm domestic care. The deep soft lavender (`#7B68EE`) serves as the active primary anchor, while `#9D8DF1` and `#E8E5F8` provide lush atmospheric tints and interactive states.
- **Secondary (`#70B8FF` / `#4A90E2` / `#E1EFFF`)**: Crisp pastel sky blue and refreshing aqua nuances signify running pure water and breezy freshness.
- **Surface & Canvas (`#FBFBFE`)**: Soft cotton white forms the primary base, offering a gentler, more natural warmth than harsh uncalibrated `#FFFFFF`.
- **Text & Neutral (`#2D3142`)**: Muted charcoal provides clear, high-legibility contrast without the stark harshness of pure pitch black.

## Typography

The typography system relies entirely on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, chosen for its friendly geometric contours, gentle humanist curvature, and pristine legibility across both marketing headers and mobile task flows. 

Larger titles feature subtle negative letter-spacing and playful bold weights that mimic bubbles and plush fabric cushions. Body text stays roomy and relaxed, utilizing ample line-height to ensure a stress-free reading experience for service menus, care instructions, and order tracking.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout embraces an airy, fluid 12-column grid system on desktop (reducing to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile). Margins and gutters scale gracefully to give elements breathing room akin to an open, sunlit laundry room:

- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns with `1.25rem` outer margins and gutters. Single-column stacked layouts dominate, ensuring thumb-friendly tap targets and vertical ease.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px)**: 8 columns with `1.5rem` margins and gutters. Two-column cards and modular ordering grids begin here.
- **Desktop (> 1024px)**: 12 columns with `3rem` margins and `2rem` gutters, maxing out at a centered container of `1200px` for optimal eye-tracking.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through soft, diffused ambient drop shadows tinted with the primary lilac hue rather than sterile grey or black:

- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas)**: Cotton white `#FBFBFE` surface with no shadow.
- **Level 1 (Cards, Rest States)**: `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(157, 141, 241, 0.12)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover States, Popovers, Modals)**: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(123, 104, 238, 0.18)`.
- **Bubbly Floating Layers**: Semi-translucent glass surfaces (`background: rgba(251, 251, 254, 0.85); backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) edged with ultra-fine borders (`1px solid rgba(232, 229, 248, 0.8)`) create clean droplet-like layers for floating order status summaries and sticky navigation bars.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary is friendly, rounded, and reminiscent of smooth soap bars and bubbling suds. Standard interactive elements and cards use `0.5rem` (`rounded`) and `1rem` (`rounded-lg`) corner radii. Specialized signature elements (such as call-to-action buttons, badge tags, and process indicators) adopt full pill shapes (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to maintain a cozy, organic rhythm throughout the product.

## Components

- **Buttons**:
  - *Primary*: Background `#7B68EE`, text `#FFFFFF`, rounded pill shape (`9999px`), with a soft lilac glow on hover (`box-shadow: 0 6px 16px rgba(123, 104, 238, 0.35)`). Active state applies a micro-scale bounce (`scale: 0.98`).
  - *Secondary / Fresh Tint*: Background `#E1EFFF`, text `#4A90E2`, rounded pill shape.
- **Cards**:
  - Clean white `#FFFFFF` surface resting on `#FBFBFE`, framed by a delicate border (`1px solid #E8E5F8`) and standard `rounded-lg` corners. Internal padding is generous (`1.5rem`), keeping laundry service pricing, weight estimators, and dry-cleaning options easily scannable.
- **Chips & Badges**:
  - Fully pill-shaped with gentle pastel fills (e.g., `#E8E5F8` with `#7B68EE` text for "Delicates", `#E1EFFF` with `#4A90E2` text for "Ready in 24h").
- **Inputs & Form Controls**:
  - Pillows of `#FFFFFF` wrapped in a `1.5px` border of `#E8E5F8`. Focus transitions to a `#70B8FF` border accompanied by an atmospheric sky-blue halo (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 4px rgba(112, 184, 255, 0.2)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios**:
  - Generously rounded selection markers with thick, friendly check indicators. Selected state fills with `#7B68EE`.
- **Order Tracker & Status Bubbles**:
  - A step-flow component using circular droplet milestones connected by soft pastel lines, illustrating garment processing stages (Washing, Drying, Ironing, Packed, Out for Delivery).
